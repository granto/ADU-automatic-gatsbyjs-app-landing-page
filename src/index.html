03 / A better way to think it through

From rough idea to a
decision you can test.

The forthcoming workflow keeps the assumptions close to the answer.

  1. 1

    Frame your decision.

    Start with your goal, the property, and the numbers you know. Identify what you still need from your builder, lender, or adviser.

  2. 2

    Build your assumptions.

    Bring a construction estimate. Test rent, financing, expenses, taxes, and timing—not just the best-case scenario.

  3. 3

    Compare. Change. Repeat.

    Look at the same property with and without the ADU. Test a higher cost, lower rent, or construction delay before relying on the result.

Ask us a question ↗
What does ADUroi calculate?

The current app is advertised as modeling ADU cash flow and financial returns, including mortgage payments, IRR, cap rate, and maximum cash used. The forthcoming redesign organizes the comparison around monthly impact and the same property with and without an ADU.

Is this a construction quote?

No. This page's sample is synthetic. The planned construction-range feature will be a planning estimate, not a bid, site inspection, or commitment from a contractor. Confirm scope, feasibility, and pricing with local professionals.

Can I use my own builder estimate?

The current product is advertised as having customizable inputs. The forthcoming workflow is designed to accept your own construction-cost assumption rather than require a generated estimate. Confirm the available input flow in the current app.

Why compare With ADU and Without ADU?

Rent alone does not show the ADU's financial effect. Comparing the same property in two scenarios helps separate the additional income from the additional costs, financing, taxes, and cash required.

Does ADUroi replace professional advice?

No. ADUroi does not replace a tax adviser, lender, appraiser, contractor, attorney, or local planning department. All results depend on assumptions. Verify zoning, permits, taxes, financing, and project costs independently.

Do I need to enter a street address here?

No. This marketing page collects no project information and its sample works without an address. The current app is a separate service; review its data requirements and policies before entering information. The redesigned estimate flow's privacy disclosures must be verified before launch.

Can professionals use ADUroi with clients?

Professional and Enterprise plans are publicly advertised. Contact us to confirm current client-use terms and limits. Branded reports and other redesigned client workflows are forthcoming, not promised current features.

Can I change assumptions and preserve earlier versions?

The current site advertises saving inputs. Named saved revisions and restoring earlier versions are part of the forthcoming redesign. Check current behavior in the app; this preview does not promise that revision history is available today.
